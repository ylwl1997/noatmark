Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

TL;DR

NoAtMark is built on a simple principle: your text stays on your device. The web tools and the Chrome extension process content entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste or scan is uploaded to our servers.

What we collect

  • Web tools & extension: No content is collected. All processing (scanning, cleaning, watermarking, inspection) happens locally in your browser.
  • API: Requests are processed transiently and are not stored. Standard Cloudflare logs may record request metadata (IP, timestamp) for security and rate limiting, in line with Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Analytics & ads: If ads are shown, an ad network (e.g. Adsterra) may set cookies and collect standard browsing data. You can control this through your browser settings and ad preferences.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't create accounts or track individual users across the site.
  • We don't read, store, or retrain on the text you process.

Third parties

The site runs on Cloudflare Pages. The API runs on Cloudflare Workers. Analytics and advertising, if present, are handled by third-party networks that follow their own privacy policies.
