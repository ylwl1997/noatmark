How it works

  • Embed. Your payload is encoded as zero-width characters and appended invisibly to your text, obfuscated with your key.
  • Publish. The text looks normal to readers — but carries your hidden mark.
  • Verify. Anyone with your key can extract the payload to prove the text is yours.
  • Remove. Paste watermarked text into the zero-width remover to strip the invisible characters.

Honest limits

  • Watermarking text isn't proof in court — it's a practical ownership signal, not a legal seal.
  • Heavy editing, reformatting, or copy-paste through some systems can drop the invisible characters.
  • Keep your key secret: anyone with the key can both verify and strip the watermark.
