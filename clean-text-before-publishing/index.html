Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I clean text before publishing?

AI-generated and web-copied text often carries invisible characters (zero-width spaces, BOMs, soft hyphens) that break formatting, validation, and SEO. Cleaning before publishing removes these artifacts so your content renders correctly everywhere.

What invisible characters appear in AI-generated text?

Zero-width spaces (U+200B), joiners (U+200C/U+200D), byte-order marks (U+FEFF), soft hyphens (U+00AD), and direction marks. They're invisible on screen but still occupy bytes and can corrupt layouts and search.

Does cleaning change the meaning of my text?

No. Cleaning only removes invisible formatting characters. Visible words, links, and structure are untouched.
